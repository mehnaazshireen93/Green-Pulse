How is the Sustainability Score Calculated?

The GreenPulse Sustainability Score (0–100) measures institutional resource efficiency against normalized national campus baselines.

Multi-Pillar Weighted Formula

Score = Σ (Normalized_Pillar_Score_i × Weight_i) / Σ (Weights)
1. Energy Efficiency (Default: 30%)

Normalized per student against 120 kWh/year benchmark.

2. Water Conservation (Default: 20%)

Normalized per capita against 45 Litres/person/day benchmark.

3. Waste Diversion (Default: 20%)

Calculates the percentage of waste diverted from open landfill to composting.

4. Green Commuting (Default: 15%)

Evaluates non-motorized, public transit, and EV shuttle modal share.

5. Carbon Intensity (Default: 15%)

Measures net CO₂e emissions per gross built area.
